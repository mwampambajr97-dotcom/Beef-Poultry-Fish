Practice as many times as it takes — not just as many samples as you can afford.

Real fillets, casings, and reagents are limited and get consumed on use. Work through each session below as many times as you need before your lab session, then bring that familiarity to the real procedure with your supervisor.

NOTE: Be sure to read all the Laboratory Safety & Health Rules.

About this simulation: these sessions are a preparation tool, built to help you arrive at each practical already familiar with the procedure, terms, and expected results. They do not replace the in-person lab — every practical below must still be completed with your supervisor.

About this lab

This simulation environment lets sudents complete practical sessions from the departmental manual on any device — no shared instrument queue, no scheduling conflicts. Readings are simulated within realistic ranges for each measurement type, so the focus stays on procedure and interpretation.
