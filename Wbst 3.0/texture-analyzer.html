Sessions / Texture Analyzer

Meat Fillet Tenderness Measurement

Measure the tenderness of a cooked meat fillet using a texture analyzer fitted with a shear blade. The instrument records force as it cuts through the sample; the peak force reached is the shear force value.

This is a preparation tool, not a substitute for the real lab. Simulated readings help you learn the procedure, terminology, and expected value ranges before your session — they cannot teach instrument handling, real biological variability, or hands-on technique. You must still complete this practical in person with your supervisor.

  1. Cook the sample to the specified internal temperature and allow it to rest.
  2. Cut a standardised core sample, parallel to the muscel fibre direction.
  3. Position the sample under the shear blade.
  4. Run the test: the blade descends at a fixed speed through the sample.
  5. Record the peak shear force from the resulting force-deflection curve.
  6. Repeat for a minimum of three core per sample.
