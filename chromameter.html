Meat Fillet Color Measurement

Measure the surface colour of a raw meat fillet using a chromameter, recorded in the L*(Lightness), a*(Redness), b*(Yellowness) colour space. Take at least three readings per sample.

This is a preparation tool, not a substitute for the real lab. Simulated readings help you learn the procedure, terminology, and expected value ranges before your session — they cannot teach instrument handling, real biological variability, or hands-on technique. You must still complete this practical in person with your supervisor.

  1. Calibrate the instrument against the white reference plate.
  2. Place the sample on a flat, evenly lit surface.
  3. Position the measuring head flush against the surface, avoiding fat or connective tissue.
  4. Take a minimum of three readings per sample, at different points.
  5. Record each reading, then review the calculated average.
